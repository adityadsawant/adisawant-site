Published paper

REVIEW PAPER · INTERNATIONAL JOURNAL OF SCIENCE AND RESEARCH (IJSR) · VOL. 15, ISSUE 9 · SEPTEMBER 2026

Artificial Intelligence in Manufacturing Quality: Applications, Challenges, and Emerging Research Directions

Aditya Sawant, Pratima Shinde · pp. 1659–1667

A review of peer-reviewed studies from January 2021 to September 2026 on how AI supports manufacturing quality, organized by quality function rather than by algorithm — from automated inspection and predictive quality to process monitoring, explainable diagnosis, quality knowledge management, and integrated decision support — along with the open challenges around data quality, model drift, explainability, and industrial validation.
